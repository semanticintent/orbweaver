# Orbweaver WebMCP challenge video

This folder preserves the production record and source artwork for Orbweaver's
sub-three-minute WebMCP Challenge video. Raw recordings, voice takes, iMovie
working files, and exported video files stay local.

## Folder map

- `PRODUCTION-RECORD.md` — final edit, export, publication, and verification record.
- `NARRATION.md` — narration used in the final cut.
- `assets/` — source title cards and the YouTube thumbnail.
- `recordings/screen/` — silent screen captures from ChatGPT and Orbweaver.
- `recordings/voice/` — narration takes recorded in short sections.
- `imovie/` — optional notes or exported iMovie project metadata.
- `exports/` — review and final MP4 exports.

## Published result

- Demo: <https://youtu.be/vEsgjJGgSOw>
- Runtime: **2:52**
- Format: **1920 × 1080, H.264 MP4**
- Audio: normalized spoken narration at approximately **-16 LUFS**

The challenge requires a video shorter than three minutes. The final video was
reviewed after YouTube processing and embedded in the submitted Devpost entry.
