# WebMCP Challenge video production record

## Outcome

The final Orbweaver demonstration was published at:

<https://youtu.be/vEsgjJGgSOw>

- Runtime: 2:52
- Canvas: 1920 × 1080
- Video: H.264 MP4
- Audio: AAC, 48 kHz, normalized to approximately -16 LUFS
- Editor: iMovie

## Demonstrated flow

1. ChatGPT discovers Orbweaver's six bounded, page-local WebMCP tools.
2. It reads the workspace and semantic proposal contract.
3. It creates an unaccepted order-fulfillment proposal from ordinary language.
4. Orbweaver validates the proposal and derives the visual layout.
5. The live workspace exposes semantic identity, relationships, and provenance.
6. ChatGPT adds Delivery tracking using an expected-revision write.
7. Acceptance remains false and export remains unavailable.
8. The person manually undoes the agent-authored revision.

## Edit

The visual cut combines an opening card, two screen recordings, and a closing
card. The slower portion of the first recording was accelerated to 4x; the
second recording remained at normal speed so its state changes stayed legible.
Narration was recorded in short sections and aligned to the visible actions.

The title cards use restrained Ken Burns movement. The YouTube thumbnail uses a
center-safe composition so the primary title remains readable in the embedded
Devpost player and on the channel page.
