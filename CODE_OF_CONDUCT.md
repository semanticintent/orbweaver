# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Orbweaver welcoming and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience,
or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this policy.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

To report conduct privately, use the repository owner's contact option on
GitHub. If that is unavailable, open an issue containing no sensitive details
and request a private reporting channel.
